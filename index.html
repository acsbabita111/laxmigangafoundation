About

A foundation shaped by the needs of real communities.

Laxmi Ganga Foundation is a registered society based in Khagaria, Bihar. Its purpose is to support community development through education, digital inclusion, livelihood-oriented capacity building, social awareness and other public-benefit initiatives.

The Foundation aims to work with a simple principle: understand local needs first, then design practical interventions that can be measured, improved and sustained.

Focus areas

Where we intend to create value

01

Education

Learning access, foundational capability, digital education and community-based learning support.

02

Digital Inclusion

Practical digital skills, safe technology use and access pathways for people in low-resource settings.

03

Livelihood Capability

Skills, information and local capacity-building that can improve employability and self-reliance.

04

Community Development

Evidence-led social initiatives built around local priorities, inclusion, awareness and public benefit.

Programmes and project areas will be expanded only after formal approval and implementation planning.
